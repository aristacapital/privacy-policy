      How it works

      Three steps, every month.

      1. Save

        On any product page, click the W tab on the right edge of the screen. The extension reads the name and price for you. On this site, type them in.

      2. Rank

        Drag what you want most to the top. The budget line settles wherever the month's money runs out.

      3. Buy

        Press Bought and the price comes off this month. The line moves up, and the list starts fresh on the 1st.
